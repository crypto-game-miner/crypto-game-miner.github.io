About Crypto Miner
⛏️ What Is Crypto Miner
Crypto Miner is a free-to-play mining simulation. You collect Game Coins through the Faucet and passive mining, level up your rig, and exchange progress for real cryptocurrency payouts via USDT or LTC. No deposit is required to start playing — every account begins with the free Faucet and a base hashrate.
🪙 COIN TYPES
Game Coins
Earned from Faucet claims and Tasks. Used to buy miners, boosts, and Season Pass rewards in the Store.
USDT Coins
Mined passively over time. Withdrawable to a real USDT wallet through FaucetPay once the minimum threshold is reached.
LTC Coins
An alternate passive-mining output. Switch your active miner between USDT and LTC output at any time in the Faucet screen.
⚙️ HOW MINING WORKS
1. Claim the Faucet regularly to earn Game Coins and a small hashrate boost.

2. Buy miners in the Store with Game Coins to raise your total hashrate.

3. Your hashrate mines USDT or LTC Coins passively, even while you're away — check back to collect your balance.

4. Complete Tasks for one-time and daily hashrate bonuses on top of your miners.
💸 WITHDRAWALS
Withdrawals are processed through FaucetPay to your USDT or LTC wallet. Minimum withdrawal amounts and processing times are shown on the Wallet page. Deposits, if you choose to top up for faster progress, are handled securely via CCPayment.
