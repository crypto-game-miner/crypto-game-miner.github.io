Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains what information Crypto Miner ("we", "the Site") collects when you use crypto-game-miner.vercel.app, and how that information is used.
📥 INFORMATION WE COLLECT
Account Data
We use anonymous authentication to create your account. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier and email address to link your progress across devices.
Wallet Addresses
When you request a withdrawal or deposit, we collect the crypto wallet address you provide, solely to process the transaction through FaucetPay (withdrawals) or CCPayment (deposits).
Gameplay Data
We store your in-game balances, mining progress, equipment level, and task completion status to keep your account state synced.
Technical Data
Our advertising partners and analytics providers may automatically collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device type, typically via cookies or similar technologies.
⚙️ HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
• To operate and maintain your account and mining progress

• To process withdrawals and deposits through FaucetPay and CCPayment

• To prevent fraud, abuse, and multi-accounting

• To display relevant advertising through our ad partners
🍪 COOKIES & ADVERTISING
We work with third-party advertising networks that may place cookies or similar tracking technologies on your device to serve and measure ads. These partners have their own privacy policies governing how they handle your data. We do not control the practices of third-party ad networks.
🔗 THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
• FaucetPay — processes withdrawals

• CCPayment — processes deposits

• Firebase — hosts account and gameplay data

• Advertising networks displaying banners on the Site
🔒 DATA RETENTION & SECURITY
We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.
👤 YOUR RIGHTS
You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us through the Advertise/Contact page.
