Frequently Asked Questions
🚰 GETTING STARTED
Do I need to pay anything to start?
No. Every account starts free with the Faucet and a base hashrate. Deposits are optional and only used to speed up progress.
How do I earn coins?
Claim the Faucet regularly, complete one-time and daily Tasks, and let your miners run passively in the background. See the About page for a full breakdown.
What's the difference between Game Coins and USDT/LTC/SOL Coins?
Game Coins are an in-game currency used to buy miners and Store items. USDT, LTC, and SOL Coins are mined passively and can be withdrawn to a real crypto wallet once you hit the minimum threshold.
⛏️ MINING & EQUIPMENT
Why did my mining stop?
Equipment health drops over time and needs to be restored. Go to the Faucet and claim to top up your equipment health and resume mining.
Can I mine more than one coin at once?
Your active miner outputs one coin type at a time — USDT, LTC, or SOL. Switch the output on the Faucet screen whenever you like.
💸 WITHDRAWALS & DEPOSITS
How do withdrawals work?
Withdrawals are processed through FaucetPay to your USDT, LTC, or SOL wallet. Minimum amounts and processing times are shown on the Wallet page.
Is depositing safe?
Deposits are handled through CCPayment, a third-party payment processor. We never store your wallet's private keys.
Why hasn't my withdrawal arrived yet?
Processing times vary by network load. If it's taken longer than the window shown on the Wallet page, contact support through the Advertise/Contact page.
📢 ADS & ADVERTISING
Why do I see ads on the site?
Ad revenue is what funds the Faucet payouts. Viewing ads and completing offers is part of how the free-to-play model works.
Can I advertise on Crypto Miner?
Yes — visit the Advertise page to purchase a banner slot directly on the site.
